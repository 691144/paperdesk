PaperDesk privacy

Last updated 11 November 2026

Short version

PaperDesk has no accounts, no servers of its own, no analytics and no ads. Your desks stay on your device. If you turn on sync, they are stored in your own Google Drive or OneDrive, and only you can see them.

What is stored where

  • On your device: your desks, notes and attachments are saved in your browser's storage for this site. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you turn on sync.
  • With sync on: PaperDesk saves a file named paperdesk-sync.json, plus one file per attachment, in a folder that only PaperDesk can use: Google Drive's hidden app-data folder, or Apps/PaperDesk in OneDrive. Your device talks to Google or Microsoft directly; the copies are never sent to anyone else.
  • Sign-in: the sign-in token from Google or Microsoft, and your account's email address (shown in the Sync dialog), are kept in your browser's storage on that device.

What PaperDesk can access

  • Google Drive: the drive.appdata permission, which covers only PaperDesk's own hidden folder. PaperDesk cannot see, read or change any of your other files. (Sending to Google Tasks or Google Docs asks for its own permission, see below.)
  • OneDrive: the Files.ReadWrite.AppFolder permission, which covers only PaperDesk's own app folder, plus the standard sign-in permissions (openid, profile, offline_access) so it can show which account is connected and stay signed in.

Google and Microsoft data is used only to save and load your own desks, is never shared, sold or used for advertising, and is never used to train AI models. PaperDesk's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Sending notes to other apps

  • When you use Send to, the notes you chose go straight from your device to the app you picked (for example OneNote, Todoist or a web address you entered). PaperDesk has no server in between and keeps no copy.
  • Optional extra permissions, asked only when you first send to that destination: Notes.Create (create OneNote pages), Tasks.ReadWrite (Microsoft To Do and Planner), https://www.googleapis.com/auth/tasks (Google Tasks) and https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file (create Google Docs; PaperDesk can see only the files it creates).
  • Tokens you paste for other tools (such as Todoist or Notion) and your saved destinations are kept in your browser's storage on that device only. They are never synced.

AI on this device

  • Speech to text, writing help and pictures run inside your browser on your own device. Your notes, recordings and prompts are never sent to PaperDesk or anyone else.
  • When you turn on an AI feature, its model files are downloaded once from Hugging Face (huggingface.co) and the AI code from jsDelivr (cdn.jsdelivr.net). These requests contain no note content. The files are stored in your browser's storage for this site, and you can delete them under AI → Models & settings.
  • PaperDesk does not use the browser's built-in speech recognition, which in some browsers sends audio to an online service.

Hosting

The app's files are served by GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep standard web server logs (such as IP addresses) when the page loads; your notes are never sent to GitHub.

Turning it off and deleting data

  • Choose Disconnect in PaperDesk's Sync dialog to stop syncing on a device.
  • Remove PaperDesk's access at myaccount.google.com/connections (Google) or account.live.com/consent/Manage (Microsoft).
  • Google: delete the hidden app data under Drive → Settings → Manage apps → PaperDesk → Delete hidden app data. OneDrive: delete the Apps/PaperDesk folder.
  • Clearing this site's data in your browser removes the copy on that device.
